COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you
make a decision.

A few useful questions to ask when arranging repair or maintenance.

Ask about your vehicle →
Can you help if I don't know what's wrong? +

Yes, this is a common place to start. Describe what you notice: sounds, smells, warning lights, changes in handling, or when the issue occurs. The shop can explain the next diagnostic step.

Will I get an estimate before work begins? +

Ask the shop to explain the estimate, diagnostic charges, and authorization process before any repair work starts. You should know what is being recommended and what it is expected to cost.

What if a repair can wait? +

Ask which findings affect safety or reliability now and which can be planned for later. The answer depends on the actual condition of the vehicle.

Can you service hybrid or electric vehicles? +

Capabilities vary by shop and vehicle. Contact the location with your year, make, and model so they can confirm whether the required service is supported.

Does sending a request confirm my appointment? +

No. A preferred date and time are a request only. The shop needs to contact you and confirm availability.
