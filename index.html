COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked
Questions

Have a question before you book? Here are a few helpful starting points.

Talk with the shop →
Do I need an appointment for service?+

An appointment helps the team plan its schedule, but availability varies. Contact the shop or submit a request to discuss your preferred day and time.

Can you help me understand which service my car needs?+

Tell the team what you are noticing, your vehicle's year, make, model, and any warning lights or changes in performance. Diagnosis may be needed before the correct repair can be identified.

Will I receive an estimate before repairs begin?+

Confirm the shop's authorization process when scheduling. You should understand the proposed work, applicable costs, and next steps before deciding to proceed.

What should I know about parts and labor warranties?+

Warranty coverage depends on the business, parts, and work performed. Ask for the current written policy and add the confirmed coverage details to this website.

Do you service hybrid and electric vehicles?+

Service capability varies by shop, model, and repair. Contact the team to confirm that they have the training and equipment needed for your specific vehicle.
