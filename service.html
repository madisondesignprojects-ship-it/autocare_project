BEFORE YOU BOOK

Helpful things
to ask first.

The details depend on the vehicle and the repair. These questions are a practical starting point.

Can you explain the recommendation? +

Ask for a plain-language explanation of the findings, why the work is recommended, and whether there are other options.

Will I know the cost before work starts? +

Ask the shop to review diagnostic charges, labor, parts, and the estimate before authorizing repair work.

How urgent is the repair? +

Ask which concerns affect safety or reliability now and which maintenance items may be planned for later. The answer depends on the vehicle's condition.
