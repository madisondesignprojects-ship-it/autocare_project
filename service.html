BEFORE YOU BOOK

Helpful to
know first.

Each vehicle and repair situation is different. These are good questions to discuss with the shop.

Will you explain what the vehicle needs?+

Ask for a clear explanation of the inspection findings, why the service is recommended, and what the next steps are before authorizing work.

Can I get an estimate first?+

Ask the shop to review the estimate and any diagnostic fees, parts, labor, and timing before work begins.

How do I know if this service is needed now?+

Share any symptoms and ask the technician to distinguish urgent safety concerns from maintenance that can be planned for later.
